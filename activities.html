Mount Eden

Found in the heart of Auckland, enjoy a nice stroll up Mount Eden to experience jaw-dropping views off the Hauraki Golf.
With one main track, people of all ages and physcial capabilities can enjoy this stunning walk.
This mountain has major cultural significance so come down and explore this track for free.

Gravity Mount Wellington

With many branches around Auckland, Gravity is a great chance for some family fun!
Enjoy this trampoline park with a large variety of foam pits, ninja courses and basketball hoops.
This attraction is suitable for people of all ages and even has a specific under 5's area to keep your little ones safe.
